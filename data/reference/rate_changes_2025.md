# Goods whose GST rate moved on 22 September 2025

**Status:** v0.9 draft, same standing as `rate_schedule.md` — the families below
are established from official announcements, but **no individual example's label
may be taken from this file**. It exists to help an annotator find candidates,
not to decide them.

---

## 1. Why this file exists

The `rate-changed-2025` tag marks examples whose applicable rate moved in the
GST 2.0 rationalisation. That slice carries the benchmark's headline finding:
a model reciting the pre-September-2025 table will be wrong specifically here,
and nowhere else so cleanly.

For advance rulings the signal is mechanical — the ruling *states* a rate, and
if it states 12% that slab no longer exists. Product listings state no rate at
all, so there is nothing to extract. This file closes that gap by recording
which **families** moved, so a listing in one of them can be surfaced as a
candidate for the annotator to confirm.

**A candidate is not a label.** The annotator still runs the full procedure in
`../guideline.md` §3: establish the heading, derive the current slab from
Notification 9/2025, and only then decide whether the rate actually moved for
this specific good. Plenty of candidates will fail that test — a family moving
does not mean every product in it moved, and packaging conditionality (§4a) can
change the answer for an individual item.

## 3. Traps in this list

**A family moving does not mean the item moved.** Many staple foods were
already at 0% or 5% before the reform. A listing for loose flour did not change
rate; a listing for pre-packaged branded flour may have. Applying the tag
because the product *sounds* like it belongs to a moved family is precisely the
error this file must not cause.

**Conditionality still governs.** `../guideline.md` §4a decides whether a food
is pre-packaged and labelled, and that test runs before any question of whether
the rate moved.

**Tobacco is explicitly excluded and stays excluded.** The official position is
that for cigarettes, zarda, unmanufactured tobacco and beedi, *"the existing
rates of GST and compensation cess will continue to apply and new rates will be
implemented at a later date."* Their rate has not settled, which is why
`guideline.md` §4d drops these families entirely rather than labelling them.

**Sources are announcements, not the instrument.** The rate that binds is the
entry in Notification 9/2025 or 10/2025. Where a press release and the
notification appear to disagree, the notification wins and this file is wrong.
